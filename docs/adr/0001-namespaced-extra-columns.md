# Namespace unknown partner columns under `extra`

When parsing partner batch files, unknown columns are placed under an `extra: dict[str, str]` field on the record rather than flat-merged onto the record dict, and the pipeline-computed fields (`validation_status`, `errors`, `extra`) are reserved — a partner column colliding with a reserved name is quarantined into `extra` under a `<name>__conflict` key. The flat-merge alternative is simpler for templates but lets a partner-supplied column named `validation_status` or `errors` silently overwrite the pipeline's own computed values, so the CSV could spoof its own validity verdict. Namespacing closes that spoofing path at the parse boundary while preserving all partner data; the cost is that templates reach unknown fields via `extra.<name>` instead of `<name>`.
